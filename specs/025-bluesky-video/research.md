# Research: Bluesky video

Phase 0 for `specs/025-bluesky-video/spec.md`. External facts come only from `docs/research/bluesky-video.md` and `docs/research/bluesky.md`; this phase cannot fetch the web. Library facts were read from the installed packages in `node_modules` (`@atproto/api` 0.22.0, `@atproto/xrpc` 0.8.14, `@atproto/common-web` 0.5.13). Code facts (F*) were read from the repository on branch `025-bluesky-video` (base `01f8296`, entries 1–6 merged). Plan decisions (P*) settle every open point in the spec.

No item in Technical Context is left as NEEDS CLARIFICATION. These external facts stay UNVERIFIED by design (the research marks them so):

- the host and auth of the upload in parts (assumed the video service and the upload token, D3, D4);
- the token audience and method for `getUploadLimits` and `getJobStatus` (D4, D5; both have a fallback, P10, P13);
- whether an app-password session may call `com.atproto.server.getSession` (used for the PDS host, with a fallback, P4);
- the shape of an "already processed" reply (accepted wherever a video blob appears, P15);
- the poll interval and total budget (D7), the duration ceiling (D2), and the codecs the service accepts (D2).

Each is kept in one named constant or function, covered by mocked tests only, and listed as an owed live check (FR-027, quickstart §7).

## Research check (constitution I)

Every external fact in the spec was compared with `docs/research/bluesky-video.md` and the installed lexicon types. They agree:

- **Embed.** `app.bsky.embed.video` has `video` (blob, `video/mp4`, at most 300,000,000 bytes), optional `captions`, `alt`, `aspectRatio` and `presentation` (`node_modules/@atproto/api/dist/client/types/app/bsky/embed/video.d.ts`). A post's embed is one union member, so one video or images.
- **Service token.** `com.atproto.server.getServiceAuth` takes `aud` (required), `exp` (Unix seconds) and `lxm`, returns `{ token }`, and has the error `BadExpiration` (`…/com/atproto/server/getServiceAuth.d.ts`). The PDS caps `exp` at 1 hour and a token without `lxm` at 1 minute (research).
- **Upload in parts.** The installed types match the research:
  - `startUpload`: JSON in `sizeBytes`, `mimeType`, `name?`, `durationMs?`, `width?`, `height?`; out `jobId`, `partSizeBytes`, `partCount`, `expiresAt`; errors `UnsupportedContentType`, `VideoTooLarge`, `VideoTooLong`, `BadAspectRatio`, `DailyLimitExceeded`, `TooManyOpenUploads`, `UploadForbidden`, `ServiceOverloaded`.
  - `uploadPart`: query `jobId`, `partNumber`; body `application/octet-stream`; out `partNumber`, `sizeBytes`. Its errors are **not in the research** but are in the installed types: `UploadNotFound`, `UploadExpired`, `InvalidPartNumber`, `PartSizeMismatch`, `UploadNotReady`, `UploadFailed`, `UploadAborted`, `UploadAlreadyCompleted`, `ServiceOverloaded`.
  - `finishUpload`: JSON in `jobId`; out `completedJobId`, `jobStatus`. Errors (installed types only): `UploadNotFound`, `UploadExpired`, `MissingParts`, `UploadNotReady`, `UnsupportedContentType`, `UploadFailed`, `UploadAborted`, `ServiceOverloaded`.
- **Job status.** `getJobStatus?jobId` returns `{ jobStatus }` with `jobId`, `did`, `state` (nine known values; "all values not listed … indicate that the job is in process"), `progress?`, `blob?`, `error?`, `failureCode?` (five known values), `message?` (`…/app/bsky/video/defs.d.ts`).
- **Upload limits.** `getUploadLimits` returns `canUpload`, `remainingDailyVideos?`, `remainingDailyBytes?`, `message?`, `error?`.
- **Limits.** 300,000,000 bytes; `video/mp4`; about 25 videos or 10 GB a day; a verified email for Bluesky-hosted accounts; duration UNVERIFIED (3 minutes conservative, 10 minutes the client ceiling); codecs unpublished (send H.264/AAC MP4).
- **Library support.** `@atproto/api` 0.22.0 includes the video lexicons but does not mint the token or call `video.bsky.app` (research). The session outputs of `createSession`, `refreshSession` and `getSession` carry an optional, loosely typed `didDoc` (`{ [_ in string]: unknown }`).
- **PDS endpoint in a DID document.** `@atproto/common-web` 0.5.13 `getPdsEndpoint(doc)` reads the `service` entry with id `#atproto_pds` and type `AtprotoPersonalDataServer` and returns its `serviceEndpoint` when it is a valid URL (`node_modules/.pnpm/@atproto+common-web@0.5.13/.../dist/did-doc.js` lines 51–82). `AtpAgent` uses it to set its `pdsUrl` after login, and leaves it unset when the document is missing, "which is the case for Bluesky's 'entryway'" (`@atproto/api/dist/atp-agent.js` lines 396–408).

The brief and the constitution list video as out of scope "until a spec says otherwise"; this spec says otherwise for Bluesky video posts.

## Code facts

- **F1 — Bluesky's step machine today.** `src/providers/bluesky/steps.ts` `stepForContent(state, content)` reads `content.text` and `content.mediaCount` only: `resolve_mentions` (when the text has mentions and `state.mentions` is absent), `upload_image_<n>` while `blobs.length < mediaCount`, then `create_post` (`mayPublish`). An unparseable state gives `invalid_state`. `index.ts` ignores `settings` and passes `content` through, so `kinds` (019) reaches it.
- **F2 — Bluesky's state.** `blueskyStateSchema` (`settings.ts`) is `{ v: 1, mentions?: Record<handle, did|null>, blobs: BlobJSON[] (default []) }`, where a blob is `{ $type: "blob", ref: { $link }, mimeType, size }`.
- **F3 — `advance` re-derives the step** from `{ text, mediaCount }` and returns `retryable_error` "The post changed while publishing; will retry." on a mismatch. It reads `pdsUrl` from the account settings and the session from the credentials (`accessJwt`, `refreshJwt`, `did`, `handle`). Every PDS call goes through `agentFor(pdsUrl, accessJwt)` (`client.ts`), a plain `Agent` with a static Bearer header, so nothing refreshes or replays behind the engine.
- **F4 — Image upload** fetches `item.url` (the public bucket URL) with `globalThis.fetch` and the step's signal, reads the whole body, then calls `uploadBlob`. No storage interface is used by providers; `Storage` (`src/server/storage/types.ts`) has no byte-range read.
- **F5 — Errors.** `errors.ts` `classify(err, kind, now, index)` maps a thrown `XRPCError` or network error: pre-send codes → retryable; 429 → retryable with `Retry-After` (capped at 24 h); for `upload` and `publish` kinds a 401 or `ExpiredToken` → retryable with `credentialsExpired`; 1, 2 or 5xx → retryable, or ambiguous for `publish`; other 4xx → fatal. `XRPCError` keeps `status`, `error`, `message` and `headers`, **not the response body** (`@atproto/xrpc/dist/types.js` lines 82–94).
- **F6 — Summaries and redaction.** The engine redacts every string leaf of the decrypted credentials from errors and summaries (`secretValues`), and `redact` **drops any summary key matching** `/token|secret|password|authorization|cookie|credential|session/i` (`src/server/scheduler/redact.ts`). A service token is not in the credentials, so the engine cannot redact it: the provider must never put it anywhere. Summary keys must avoid those words or they vanish.
- **F7 — Step results.** `applyStepResult` (`src/server/scheduler/record.ts`): `continue` resets `attemptCount` to 0, **clears `lastError`** and sets `nextAttemptAt` to `notBefore` (or now); `retryable_error` increments `attemptCount` and fails the target at `PUBLISH_MAX_ATTEMPTS` (default 5). So a long provider wait must be a `continue`, and a `continue` cannot show why it waits.
- **F8 — Wait messages today.** The target page shows `lastError` (`src/server/services/views/post.ts`). The engine's own deferrals (publish limit, creation allowance) set `lastError` to a wait message on a target that is still scheduled or publishing (`publishing.ts` lines 198–245), so a wait message in `lastError` is an established pattern.
- **F9 — The engine's ceilings.** A target whose first step was leased more than `PUBLISH_MAX_DURATION_HOURS` ago (default 24, range 1–168) is failed "Publishing did not complete." before any step runs (`publishing.ts` line 171). `firstStepAt` is set on the first lease and never reset by a `continue`. Every provider call is bounded by `SCHEDULER_PROVIDER_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` (default 10, 1–20) through one `AbortSignal` for the whole `advance`.
- **F10 — Creation allowance (G22).** The engine reads `StepInfo.allowance`: `units` on a first lease, `retryUnits` when `attemptCount > 0`, nothing when the value is 0. When the window cannot take the units it defers the target before leasing, with "Waiting for <name> (<used> of <count> used in the last <window>); nothing was created." and no provider call. `count` must be at least 11 (`assertCreationAllowance`).
- **F11 — Publish media for a video.** `resolvePublishMedia` (`src/server/services/media-variants.ts`) gives the provider the stored original when the plan is "original", or the ready version's `publicUrl`, `byteSize`, `width`, `height` and probed facts when it is "derive"; it waits (`notReady`) while the version is built or missing. So `item.url` and `item.bytes` name exactly the file to send, and they change when the version is rebuilt.
- **F12 — Post type.** `resolvePostType`: one video with no declared choice → `video`; two or more items → `carousel`, whose limits are `videoLimitsFor(caps, "carousel")`, i.e. the base block when there is no carousel override. So two videos, or a video and an image, are refused by the base block's `maxVideos: 1` and `withImages: false` (`too_many_videos`, `video_with_images`).
- **F13 — Generic validator messages.** `too_many_videos`: "The post has 2 videos; the limit is 1."; `video_with_images`: "This account does not accept a video together with images." (`src/providers/validation.ts` lines 229–241). Threads and Instagram reword video issues in their own `validate`.
- **F14 — Requirements summary.** `requirementsOf` takes `video.notes` from `byPostType[shown].notes`, where `shown` is `video` for one video with no choice. `video.postType` is non-null only with a declared choice (023 P2), so no "Post as" row is shown for Bluesky. `adapts` gets "cut to 3 minutes" and "rewrapped or re-encoded" from the declared limits; `cannot` gets "cannot be made smaller than 300 MB". Labels: `durationLabel(180)` = "3 minutes", `videoBytesLabel(300_000_000)` = "300 MB".
- **F15 — One existing assertion pins Bluesky's empty video notes.** `src/providers/requirements.test.ts` "leaves providers without choices or per-type limits unchanged" loops over `["mock", "bluesky", "x"]` and expects `video.notes` to be `[]`. FR-018 requires two notes, so that loop must drop `bluesky` (P20). Every other existing test that touches Bluesky video filters providers by `maxVideos === 0` at run time (`media-fit.test.ts`, `compose/check.test.ts`, `tests/helpers/limit-rows.ts`), so it moves to X on its own.
- **F16 — Test doubles.** `tests/helpers/fake-pds.ts` routes on method and pathname only and records `url`, headers and body; the video service's paths (`/xrpc/app.bsky.video.*`) differ from the PDS's, so one fake serves both and tests assert the host from `url`. Bluesky integration tests stub `fetch` and serve `https://media.example.test/<key>` from the in-memory storage with a whole-body 200 (`tests/integration/bluesky/images.test.ts`); a byte-range server does not exist yet. `createVideoAsset` (`tests/helpers/factories.ts`) builds a video row from facts without ffmpeg.
- **F17 — Limits inventory.** `tests/integration/docs/limits-inventory.test.ts` checks `docs/limits.md` against the registered capabilities and allowances; `tests/integration/limits/enforcement.test.ts` generates `<key>: <category>` tests from capabilities. Facebook already has a notes-only `byPostType.video` entry, so such an entry needs no inventory change.
- **F18 — Docket's PDS address.** `settings.pdsUrl` is the address the person entered (default `https://bsky.social`, Bluesky's entryway, which proxies to the account's real PDS). The credentials do not hold the DID document.

## Plan decisions

- **P1 — Declaration.** `src/providers/bluesky/capabilities.ts` (new) exports:
  - `BLUESKY_VIDEO = { maxVideos: 1, withImages: false, containers: ["mp4"], videoCodecs: ["h264"], audioCodecs: ["aac"], silentAllowed: true, maxBytes: 300_000_000, maxDurationSeconds: 180 }` (D2);
  - `video: { ...BLUESKY_VIDEO, byPostType: { video: { notes: [two notes] } } }` — a notes-only entry, as Facebook has, so the summary shows them (D13);
  - `postTypes: ["text", "image", "carousel", "video"]` and no `postTypeChoices` (D1);
  - `BLUESKY_VIDEO_ALLOWANCE = { count: 25, windowSeconds: 86_400, name: "Bluesky's daily video upload allowance" }` on the provider (D6).
  Text, image and publish-limit values move into the same file unchanged; `index.ts` imports them. No other bound is declared, so nothing else refuses.
- **P2 — The allowance is reserved where the upload begins.** FR-005 says the start-upload step reserves it, and US4 #6 says a spent allowance makes the target wait "with no Bluesky call". Both hold only if the reservation sits on the first step of an upload, which is the limits check (D5 puts it before the start). So:
  - `check_upload_limits` reserves `{ units: 1, retryUnits: 0 }` when no limit wait is in progress (a retry of a read creates nothing);
  - `start_upload` reserves `{ units: 0, retryUnits: 1 }`: nothing on its first lease (the check already reserved), one more on a retry, since a start that timed out may have opened an upload (D6's "one again on a retry of that step");
  - the hourly re-checks of a limit wait reserve nothing, so a 23-hour wait costs one unit, not 24;
  - an upload restarted after expiry reserves again (it is a new upload).
  This keeps D6's count and US4 #6's "no Bluesky call" (the deferral happens before the limits check is leased). A post with mentions still resolves them first, as for images. Recorded against the letter of FR-005.
- **P3 — Service tokens.** One helper, `serviceToken(env, aud, lxm)`, calls `com.atproto.server.getServiceAuth` on `settings.pdsUrl` with the session's access JWT (the entryway proxies it, as for every other PDS call), with `exp = floor(now / 1000) + 300` from the engine clock (D4's "at most 5 minutes"). The token lives in one local variable of the step, is sent only as `Authorization: Bearer …` to `https://video.bsky.app`, and is scrubbed from every message the step builds. The audience and method per call:

  | Call | `aud` | `lxm` | Verified? |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `getUploadLimits` | `did:web:video.bsky.app` | `app.bsky.video.getUploadLimits` | UNVERIFIED (research "pattern") |
  | `startUpload`, `uploadPart`, `finishUpload` | `did:web:<PDS host>` | `com.atproto.repo.uploadBlob` | tutorial and official client (host for the parts calls UNVERIFIED) |
  | `getJobStatus` | `did:web:video.bsky.app` | `app.bsky.video.getJobStatus` | UNVERIFIED (D4); fallback P13 |

- **P4 — The PDS host.** The limits-check step calls `com.atproto.server.getSession` (installed types: `didDoc?` in its output) once per upload and reads the `#atproto_pds` service the way the installed `getPdsEndpoint` does (id `#atproto_pds`, type `AtprotoPersonalDataServer`, `serviceEndpoint` an `https:` URL), keeping only its hostname. `@atproto/common-web` is not a direct dependency (pnpm does not expose it to Docket), so `pds-host.ts` reads the documented JSON shape in about ten lines rather than importing it; this reads data, it does not replace a package. If `getSession` is refused with a 4xx other than a rejected session, or the document has no usable entry, the host of `settings.pdsUrl` is used. The host and its source (`session` or `configured`) are kept in the upload state (not secret) so every step of one upload uses the same audience, and recorded in the attempt log. A rejected session (401, `ExpiredToken`) is refreshed as today. *Why not store it at connect or refresh:* the credentials schema would change, accounts connected before this entry would lack it, and the engine redacts every credentials string, so the host would turn every audience in the attempt log into "[redacted]".
- **P5 — A small fetch client for the video service, not an `Agent`.** `video-service.ts` calls `https://video.bsky.app/xrpc/<method>` with `globalThis.fetch`, the step's signal and exact headers, and parses each reply with Zod. A non-2xx reply throws `VideoServiceError`, a subclass of `XRPCError` (so `classify`, `statusOf`, `errorName` and `rateLimitNotBefore` apply unchanged) that also carries the parsed JSON body. *Why:* `XRPCError` drops the body, and the "already processed" reply carries its blob there (research; P15); the part body and `Content-Type: application/octet-stream` must be exact. `VIDEO_SERVICE_URL` and `VIDEO_SERVICE_DID` are constants (FR-030: no configurable service).
- **P6 — Reading one part from storage.** Each step reads its bytes with `fetch(item.url, { headers: { Range: "bytes=<first>-<last>" }, signal })` on the media's public URL, the address images are already fetched from (F4). The reply must be `206` with exactly the expected length and a `Content-Range` whose total equals the declared size; a `200` is cancelled unread (so a store that ignores ranges never loads the whole file) and the step is retried with "The video's storage did not return the requested part (HTTP 200; byte ranges are needed); will retry." A total that differs from the declared size restarts the upload (P14). Before starting, `bytes=0-0` reads the total and compares it with `item.bytes` (D10); a mismatch retries the start with "The stored video's size does not match its record; will retry." No `Storage` change and no new dependency. That the public bucket URL honours ranges is an owed operator check (S3-compatible stores do).
- **P7 — Parts.** Part `k` (from 1) covers bytes `(k − 1) × partSizeBytes` to `min(k × partSizeBytes, sizeBytes) − 1`. The start reply is usable only when `partSizeBytes ≥ 1`, `1 ≤ partCount ≤ 10,000`, `(partCount − 1) × partSizeBytes < sizeBytes ≤ partCount × partSizeBytes` and `expiresAt` parses; otherwise the start is retried as an unusable answer (the opened upload expires unused). `partsSent` advances only on a 2xx whose `partNumber` matches.
- **P8 — Step names.** `check_upload_limits`, `start_upload`, `upload_part_<k>`, `finish_upload`, `check_job` (repeated), `create_post`. Only `create_post` may publish. Text and image posts keep `resolve_mentions`, `upload_image_<n>`, `create_post` with identical requests (US1 #9).
- **P9 — The limit wait.** `canUpload: false` or `DailyLimitExceeded` returns `continue` with the upload back in its limits phase, `limitWaitSince` set on the first refusal, `notBefore` one hour later and a wait message (P11). Because the engine fails a target 24 hours after its first step by default (F9), and the first step is at or before the first refusal, a refusal at 24 hours could never be seen. So the refusal that comes **23 hours or more** after the first one fails the target with Bluesky's message: the wait covers 24 hourly checks and ends before the engine's ceiling, so the person sees why. A lower `PUBLISH_MAX_DURATION_HOURS` ends it sooner with the engine's "Publishing did not complete."; nothing is published either way. Recorded against the letter of D5's "24 hours".
- **P10 — A refused limits check is skipped.** A 4xx other than 429 from `getUploadLimits` (including 401 and 403, since its token is UNVERIFIED), or a refused `getServiceAuth` for it other than a rejected session, records `limitsCheck: "skipped"` with the code, and the upload starts. A 429, 5xx, timeout or dropped connection is retried. An unreadable 2xx is also skipped (the start's `DailyLimitExceeded` stays authoritative).
- **P11 — G24: a `continue` may say why it waits.** `StepResult`'s `continue` gains an optional `wait?: string`, recorded (redacted) as the target's `lastError` instead of `null`, so a waiting target shows the reason, as the engine's own deferrals do (F8). It uses no attempt. Existing providers never set it, so they are unchanged (pinned by a `record.test.ts` case). Only Bluesky's limit wait uses it. Changes: `src/providers/types.ts`, `src/server/scheduler/record.ts`, the generic hooks index and §7 of `docs/adding-a-provider.md`. *Reverse:* drop the member; waits would show nothing. *Rejected:* `retryable_error` with `notBefore` (Threads' quota wait) uses an attempt per hour and fails after 5.
- **P12 — Poll pace.** Constants in `video-state.ts`: `FIRST_READ_DELAY_MS = 30_000`, `FAST_READ_INTERVAL_MS = 60_000`, `FAST_PHASE_MS = 600_000`, `SLOW_READ_INTERVAL_MS = 300_000`, `PROCESSING_CEILING_MS = 1_800_000`, `MAX_JOB_READS = 16`. The finish step sets `finishedAt` and returns `notBefore = finishedAt + 30 s`. After each read without a blob, the next is due at `readAt + 60 s` if the read was before `finishedAt + 10 min`, else `readAt + 5 min`. A read made at or after `finishedAt + 30 min`, or the 16th read, that has no blob fails the target with D7's message; so the last read is made at the ceiling, never skipped. With 30 s ticks the reads fall near 0:30, 1:30 … 9:30, 10:30, 15:30, 20:30, 25:30 and 30:30: 15 reads, failing by about 31 minutes plus tick lag (SC-005's 35 minutes).
- **P13 — Job status auth fallback.** The status read sends a token (D4). If the video service refuses it with 401 or 403, `statusAuth` becomes `none` and the next read (at the normal pace, counted) is sent without a token; if that is refused too, the target fails with "Bluesky refused to report the video's processing status (<code>); nothing was published." *Why:* the token's audience for this call is UNVERIFIED, and a wrong guess must not fail every Bluesky video after it is uploaded, the same reasoning D5 applies to the limits check.
- **P14 — Restarts.** One counter, `restarts` (0–2), covers every way an upload is lost before it finishes: `expiresAt` passed (checked before each part and the finish, with a 60-second margin, with no call), and the replies `UploadExpired`, `UploadNotFound`, `UploadAborted`, `PartSizeMismatch` and `MissingParts`, and a stored size that no longer matches (P6). A restart returns `continue` with a fresh upload in its limits phase (keeping `mentions` and `restarts + 1`); the old upload expires unused. A third loss fails with D11's message for expiry, or "Bluesky lost the upload before it finished (<code>); nothing was published." for the others. `UploadAlreadyCompleted` on a part moves on to `finish_upload`. `UploadNotReady` and `ServiceOverloaded` are retried. `UploadFailed` and `InvalidPartNumber` fail.
- **P15 — A blob arrives early.** A finish reply whose `jobStatus.blob` is present, or any non-2xx reply from `startUpload` or `finishUpload` whose JSON body carries a video blob at `jobStatus.blob` or `blob` (the research's "already processed" reply, shape UNVERIFIED), goes straight to `create_post` with that blob and no status read. A blob is usable when it parses as `{ $type: "blob", ref: { $link }, mimeType: "video/mp4", size ≥ 1 }`.
- **P16 — A failed state wins.** A status with `state: JOB_STATE_FAILED` fails the target even if it also carries a blob; any other state with a blob completes the job; any other state without a blob, an unknown state, or an unreadable 2xx means still processing (counted as a read). D7 does not say which wins when both appear; a failed job's blob is not trusted.
- **P17 — Explanations.** `video-errors.ts` holds the plain text for each start code and failure code of D8 (contract §7), matched exactly on the code. Bluesky's `message` is sanitised (control characters removed, at most 200 characters) and every token value of the step replaced with `[redacted]` before it is shown.
- **P18 — Fitting the saved state.** `fitState(state, content)` is shared by `stepFor` and `advance`:
  - images in the post and a `video` member in the state, or one video and image blobs in the state → a fresh `{ v: 1 }` (the post changed);
  - an upload whose fields are inconsistent for its phase (contract §2) → unreadable, `invalid_state`, fatal as today; nothing can have been published before `create_post`.
  `advance` also compares the saved `url` and `sizeBytes` with the post's video; a difference returns `continue` with `{ v: 1 }` and `notBefore` now, so the next tick starts again from the first step (D12).
- **P19 — Bluesky wording for refused mixes.** `validateBluesky` rewords `too_many_videos` to "Bluesky takes one video per post; this post has <n>." and `video_with_images` to "Bluesky takes one video per post with no images alongside it." (US3 #5), and leaves every other issue unchanged (FR-004).
- **P20 — One existing assertion changes.** `src/providers/requirements.test.ts` "leaves providers without choices or per-type limits unchanged" loops over `["mock", "x"]` instead of `["mock", "bluesky", "x"]`, and a new case pins Bluesky's notes and `postType: null`. FR-018 requires the notes, which SC-008's "unchanged" cannot coexist with for this one generic loop. No file under `src/providers/bluesky/` or `tests/integration/bluesky/` changes, so every existing Bluesky test passes unchanged. The secrets check for video goes in a new `tests/integration/bluesky/video-no-secrets.test.ts`, not in the existing `no-secrets.test.ts`.
- **P21 — Timeouts on a part.** A part whose call is aborted by the step's signal returns "Sending part <k> of <n> to Bluesky did not finish within the provider time limit (SCHEDULER_PROVIDER_TIMEOUT_SECONDS); will retry." so a part that never fits the limit ends, at `PUBLISH_MAX_ATTEMPTS`, with a message naming the setting (spec Assumptions). If the engine's own timer wins the race, its generic "The provider call timed out." is shown; both are retryable.
- **P22 — No compose or env change.** `docker-compose.yml` and `.env.example` are unchanged (FR-025): the video service address is a constant, and ffmpeg, the worker and the upload limits arrived with entries 2 and 6.
