# Getting started

A project is one place to run your social posting: its connected accounts, its posts and its team, all in one time
zone. This guide takes you from an empty project to a first scheduled post. It takes about five minutes to read.

## 1. Connect an account

Open **Accounts** and choose **Add an account**. Pick the platform, then choose the Pages or profiles you want to post
to. Only owners and admins can connect accounts. See [Members and accounts](accounts.md) for connecting an account that
belongs to someone else.

## 2. Add posting slots

A posting slot is a weekly time for one account, for example Tuesdays at 9:00. Add slots on the **Accounts** page.
**Add to queue** puts a post in the next free slot. **Schedule** and **Publish now** don't need any slots.

## 3. Write your first post

Open **Compose**, write your text and pick the accounts. Then choose **Add to queue**, **Schedule** or **Publish now**.
Afterwards, choose **See it on the calendar** to check where it landed.

## Optional next steps

### Brand voice and generating posts

Set up a voice profile so generated posts sound like you. Use **Generate** for a single batch of ideas, or **Batch jobs**
for many at once. Generated posts wait in **Review** until someone approves them. See
[Generator and jobs](generator.md).

### Media

Add images and videos in **Media**. Instagram and TikTok posts always need media. The other platforms accept text-only
posts. [Platform limits](limits.md) lists which kinds and sizes each platform takes.

### Invite teammates

Go to **Settings**, then **Members**, to invite people.

## Roles

- **Owner**: full control, including inviting owners, changing roles and transferring ownership.
- **Admin**: everything an editor can do, plus accounts, posting slots, brand voice, settings and invitations. Can't
  invite owners or change members' roles.
- **Editor**: writes, schedules and generates posts, and uploads media. Can't connect accounts or change posting slots,
  brand voice or settings.

## Where to look afterwards

- **Calendar** shows what is scheduled and when.
- **Posts** lists every post and its status.
- **Review** holds generated posts waiting for approval.
- **Failures** lists posts that didn't publish, and lets you retry them. See [Failures and retrying](failures.md).
- **Activity** shows what happened to publishing. See [Activity](activity.md).
- The notifications bell flags problems as they happen.
